Product development

Hamar: an electric bike built by four students

In our first year at SSIPMT Raipur, four of us decided the future wouldn't run on petrol and diesel. We wanted to build a car, but funds were short, so we built a cost-effective electric bike instead. It took three years. We named it Hamar, Chhattisgarhi for "ours".

100 kmrange on one charge
₹7to fully charge
45 km/htop speed
250 kgload capacity
25°+climbs where most e-bikes stop at 20°
₹60ktarget price in bulk, vs ₹1.25 lakh market

The bike

Smooth and powerful, with a steel frame, a custom motor and a long-range lithium-ion battery. Noise-free and pollution-free, made for city commutes and weekend rides. The team is now working to push it to 75 km/h.

Team: Sagar Sahu, Kunal Urkude, Jaspreet Singh Hanspal and Bhuvesh Sahu

Also built: Dyno e-cycle
